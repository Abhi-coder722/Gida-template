## Prototype assumptions

The website therefore treats the online shop as a proposal prototype, not as verified live inventory. Products, prices, deals, testimonials, and service copy are deliberately worded as concept/demo content. The assortment direction is based on the business category and a realistic international neighborhood supermarket in Frankfurt: fresh produce, spices, rice and pulses, bakery, chilled/fresh foods, frozen goods, beverages, snacks, and international specialties.

## Design direction derived from research

- Local Frankfurt neighborhood supermarket, not a national grocery chain.
- German-first interface, with small multilingual/international cues.
- Warm, practical, premium-but-affordable visual tone.
- Emphasize long opening hours as a verified strength.
- Use representative grocery photography with authentic market, produce, spices, grains, and packaged-food atmosphere.
